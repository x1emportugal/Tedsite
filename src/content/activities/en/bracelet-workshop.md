The bracelet workshop is the quietest activity in the repertoire, and that is exactly what makes it useful: it lands well after the games, when the group needs to come down before the cake.

## How it runs

I set up a table with beads in a range of colours, shapes and sizes, elastic cord and clasps. Each child picks their own and builds the bracelet their way. I help where needed, mostly the younger ones with the final knot.

There is no model to copy and no right answer — which is why it works. Every bracelet comes out different and every child takes theirs home.

## Mixed-age groups

When there is a wide age range in the same group I bring two sets of materials: large beads and thick cord for the youngest, smaller beads and more intricate patterns for the older ones. Nobody finds it too easy or too hard.
